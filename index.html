Why We Exist

We saw
a problem.

Most clothing is working against you.

The average wardrobe is full of synthetic fabric — polyester, nylon, spandex — marketed as performance, sold as premium, and priced like it. What most people don't know is that those same materials are endocrine disruptors. They interfere with your hormones. And you're wearing them all day.

DISCERN started as a simple refusal. We traced every link in the supply chain — from American-grown cotton fields to domestic mills to cut-and-sew shops stateside — and built a tee with nowhere left to cut corners. No imports. No synthetics. No compromise on how it feels.

This is a closet staple. Something you reach for because it can be dressed up, down, even sideways too if you want. We're building it for the person who already knows better — and for everyone else we can bring along.
